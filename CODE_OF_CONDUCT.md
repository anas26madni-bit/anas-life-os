# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation must remain respectful, professional, inclusive, and evidence-focused. Contributors should communicate clearly, accept constructive review, protect user privacy, and treat accessibility and security concerns seriously.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, disclosure of private information, unsafe handling of security reports, and knowingly misrepresenting test or approval evidence.

## Engineering conduct

Contributors must not bypass approved requirements, quality gates, security controls, architecture boundaries, or sprint authorization. Disagreement should be resolved with traceable evidence and explicit approval rather than silent compromise.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Security-sensitive reports must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public discussion. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented, and handled privately where practical.
